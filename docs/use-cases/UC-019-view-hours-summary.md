# UC-019: View hours totals

- **Status:** Approved
- **Phase:** 1
- **Primary actor:** Partner
- **Replaces:** sums of the hours sheet
- **Business rules:** BR-083

## Goal
See each partner's hours per year and per month.

## Trigger
Year-end or meeting.

## Preconditions
- None.

## Main flow
1. Partner opens **Hours → Totals**: per partner the year total and 12 monthly totals; grand total.
2. Partner taps a month to see the entries.

## Alternative flows
- **A1 – Change year**.

## Error flows
- None.

## Postconditions
- None.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1: Monthly totals add up to the year total; partners add up to the grand total.
- [ ] AC-2: Matches the hours on the year page.
